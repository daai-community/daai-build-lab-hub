# Final Submission Guide & Deliverable Checklist

All teams must submit their completed deliverables by the stated deadline. Before submitting the Final Submission Form, verify that every item below is complete and accessible without login walls.

---

## 1. Public GitHub Repository
* Repository visibility is switched from **Private** to **Public**.
* `README.md` clearly states the business problem, key findings, architecture overview, live demo link, and local setup instructions.
* `requirements.txt` (or `environment.yml` / `package.json`) is included and tested.
* Zero API keys, passwords, or `.env` files are exposed in commit history.

## 2. Completed Decision Log (`DECISION_LOG.md`)
* Includes documented data cleaning assumptions, engineering, KPI formulas, model/tool trade-offs, and known limitations.

## 3. Live Interactive & Accessible Working Solution
* Evaluators and users must be able to open and interact with your solution directly in a browser without running local code.
* Examples include:
  * A published, publicly viewable **Power BI**, **Tableau Public**, or **Looker Studio** dashboard.
  * A deployed **Streamlit**, **Gradio**, **Hugging Face Space**, or web application.
  * A live hosted API endpoint or interactive automation demo with a usable frontend.

## 4. Video Walkthrough (3 to 5 Minutes)
* Hosted on YouTube (Unlisted or Public), Loom, or Google Drive (set to "Anyone with the link can view").
* Covers: (1) The business problem and data insights uncovered, (2) A live walkthrough of the working dashboard/app, and (3) Key engineering trade-offs and business recommendations.
