# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### General Program

**What is DaAI Build Lab?**
DaAI Build Lab is a 2-week collaborative sprint designed to give Data and AI practitioners hands-on experience working in cross-functional product teams on realistic industry problems.

**How much time does the sprint require?**
Participants should commit a minimum of **5 to 8 hours per week** over the two-week sprint to collaborate with teammates, attend check-ins, and ship their team's deliverable.

**Is there a cost to participate?**
No. DaAI Build Lab is completely free for selected participants.

---

### Teams & Workflow

**How are teams structured?**
Each team consists of 5 builders: **3 Data Track practitioners** (Data Analysis, BI, SQL, Data Engineering) and **2 AI Track practitioners** (ML Engineering, LLM/RAG workflows, AI Automation).

**Where does our team host its code?**
Each team's Team Lead creates a private GitHub repository under their own account on Day 1 and adds all teammates as collaborators. At the end of the sprint, teams switch their repository to **Public** for evaluation and portfolio showcasing.

**What tools or cloud platforms are we required to use?**
None. Teams choose their own open-source and free-tier stack (Python, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, Streamlit, Hugging Face, n8n, Vercel, etc.) and justify those choices in their Decision Log.

---

### Deliverables, Showcases & Certificates

**What does our team need to submit at the end of the sprint?**
Every team must submit four core items:
1. A public GitHub repository with clean, reproducible code and a thorough `README.md`.
2. A completed `DECISION_LOG.md` documenting key data and engineering trade-offs.
3. A **live, accessible working solution** that evaluators and users can interact with directly (such as a published Power BI/Tableau dashboard, a hosted Streamlit/Gradio web app, or a live interactive prototype).
4. A **3 to 5 minute video walkthrough** demonstrating the business insights and working solution end-to-end.

**How are projects evaluated?**
Projects are scored across four pillars:
1. **Business Impact & Problem Framing (30%)**
2. **Data Quality & Analytical Rigor (25%)**
3. **AI / Technical Execution & Reproducibility (25%)**
4. **Cross-Track Collaboration & Decision Log Clarity (20%)**

**Who gets featured in the public showcase?**
Every team that submits a complete, reproducible solution and Decision Log is indexed permanently in the cohort showcase archive. The **Top 3 highest-scoring teams** receive dedicated social media spotlights and live showcase features.

**Do participants receive a certificate?**
Yes. Verified **Certificates of Completion** are issued strictly to builders who ship their team's final deliverable and demonstrate active, verifiable GitHub commit contributions during the sprint.
